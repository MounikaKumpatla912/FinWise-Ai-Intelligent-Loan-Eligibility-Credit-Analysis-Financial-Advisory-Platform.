FAQ

Common questions

Is my financial data stored anywhere?
Eligibility check results are optionally logged to a Google Sheet you control, purely for your own records. Nothing is shared with third parties.
How is the eligible loan amount calculated?
As a baseline, we use 20× your verified monthly salary, adjusted by risk factors like credit score, existing EMI load, and age.
Does the AI advisor replace a real financial advisor?
No — it's a starting point for financial literacy and planning. For high-stakes decisions, always consult a licensed professional.
What credit score range does the analyzer use?
300–649 is Poor, 650–749 is Good, and 750–900 is Excellent — matching common Indian credit bureau scales.
