About FinWise AI

Financial clarity, without the jargon

We build tools that turn opaque lending decisions into transparent, explainable numbers — backed by AI you can actually question.

Our Mission

Give every applicant the same clarity a bank's risk team has — before they apply, not after a rejection.

Our Approach

Transparent rules, explainable risk scoring, and an AI advisor that shows its reasoning instead of hiding it.

Our Journey

How FinWise AI came together

2023 — The idea

Frustrated by opaque loan rejections, our founding team began prototyping a transparent eligibility engine.

2024 — First risk model

Launched a rules-based risk classifier covering credit score, income, and existing debt load.

2025 — AI advisor added

Integrated a conversational AI advisor grounded in each user's own financial snapshot.

2026 — FinWise AI platform

Unified eligibility, credit analysis, EMI planning, and advisory into a single fintech dashboard.
